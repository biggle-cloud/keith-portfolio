Meet Martin

Meet the chef

Cooked as
the tide allows.

Meet Martin Williams — chef, co-owner, and the reason the regulars keep coming back. For more than thirty years he has run the kitchen at Joplins with one simple aim: consistently excellent food, served in a relaxed and friendly room.

Martin cooks around the daily-changing seafood that arrives from Hodgsons of Hartlepool, so the specials follow the boats rather than the calendar. Meat comes from Petch's, the area's award-winning butchers — which is why the fillet steak and duck hold their own alongside the fish. Martin and Gill Williams have built Joplins together, and three decades on, they're still enjoying every service.
