Hire questions

Do you supply an operator?

Yes. Most of our hires are operated: the machine comes with a GNAT UK operator trained on the Brokk fleet, working to a method agreed with you beforehand. We can also supply a machine on its own where your team has trained operators and the job suits it – ask and we will talk it through.

Is there a minimum hire period?

No fixed minimum. We price around the job – a few days, a call-off under a framework, or a long programme – and agree the duration at the same time as the method.

Do you provide training?

Operated hires need none: our operators arrive qualified. If you take a machine on its own and your team needs training, we agree what is required before delivery, so nothing is left to the first morning on site.

How do I know which machine I need?

Tell us the smallest opening on the way in, any floor-loading limits and what has to come out. We'll recommend a machine and set-up – and say so if a different method would do the job better.

Do you work anywhere in the UK?

Yes. We mobilise nationwide, including to water and AMP8 schemes, COMAH sites and live operational facilities, and we check access and programme constraints before the machine sets off.

Need a machine on site?

Tell us the job, the access and the dates – or that you're looking to buy. Prefer to talk? Call 01748 826046.
