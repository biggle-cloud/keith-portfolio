Hire questions

Do you provide operators with machine hire?

Yes – operated hire is the default. Our operators are qualified on the Brokk fleet and bring a method-led approach with them. Plant-only supply is considered case by case, where the receiving site has the operator capability and the work suits it.

What's the minimum hire period?

Hire is built around the works package, not a fixed minimum. Short-duration packages, framework call-offs and longer programmes are all supplied – we'll define duration alongside the method so the machine and the team are matched to the scope.

Is training included?

For operated hire, the operator is qualified and competent on arrival. For plant-only supply where training is needed, we define what is required as part of mobilisation rather than leave it to the receiving site to discover on day one.

I'm not sure which size machine I need.

Most enquiries start there. We help you identify the right machine, the right set-up and, where needed, the right method – based on access, structural behaviour and the work itself, not just output or weight class.

Can you mobilise nationally?

Yes – the fleet is mobilised across the UK, including water and AMP8 infrastructure, COMAH sites and live operational facilities. Programme and access constraints are reviewed up front so the machine arrives able to start.
