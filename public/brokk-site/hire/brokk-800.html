Brokk 800 hire

For the heaviest structures, with the operator kept well back. The biggest remote-controlled machine Brokk makes: around 11 tonnes, with a 10 m arm and a 1.2-tonne MB 1200 breaker. It deals with massive foundations and heavy steelwork while the operator stays at a safe distance.
